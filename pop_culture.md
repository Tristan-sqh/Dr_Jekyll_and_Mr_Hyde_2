# Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde in Pop culture

The impact this story has had over the years has been nothing short of tremendous. While many may not know the original book, it is virtually impossible to have never interacted with any of the works in influenced. The Hulk's conception for example is likely took inspiration from both *Strange case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde* and *Frankenstein's Monster*. Music lovers may know the song by the Who called *Dr. Jekyll & Mr. Hyde*. Stil in music but more likely to have been listened to recently by members of Gen Z, if likely not on purpose, was the song of the same name by Ezra Furman which played in the pilot episode of the series *Sex Education*.
