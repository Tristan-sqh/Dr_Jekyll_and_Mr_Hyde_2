# The history behind the book

The book has been studied quite a lot throughout the years, hence the many different interpretations. It may have been about Victorian society and the evil hidden beneath the surface, it may have been influenced by Robert Louis Stevenson's own experience as a friend of [Eugène Chantrelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Elizabeth_Chantrelle) who murdered his own wife in 1878 and is believed to have commited other murders in France, it may have been about repressed homesexuality, or it may simply have been a story he dreamt while bedridden and severely ill.
We will likely never get definite answers to this question, especially since it is likely that the truth is a mix of all the frequently discussed interpretation as an author is at the end of the day but a human being with their own unique life experiences that shape their mind
