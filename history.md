# The history behind the book

The book has been studied quite a lot throughout the years, hence the many different interpretations. It may have been about Victorian society and the evil hidden beneath the surface. It may also have been influenced by Robert Louis Stevenson's own experience as a friend of [Eugène Chantrelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Elizabeth_Chantrelle), who murdered his own wife in 1878 and is believed to have committed other murders in France. It could also have been about repressed homosexuality, or it may simply have been a story he dreamt while bedridden and severely ill.
We will likely never get a definite answer to this question, especially since it is likely that the truth is a mix of all the frequently discussed interpretation as an author is, at the end of the day, but a human being with their own unique life experiences that shape their mind
